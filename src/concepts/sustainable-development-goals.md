# Sustainable Development Goals

The UN 2030 Agenda provides the **policy "how"** that complements the human-rights "why" for AI-enabled healthy longevity:

| SDG | Relevance |
|---|---|
| **SDG 3** (Good Health & Well-being) | "Healthy lives and well-being at all ages"; Target 3.4 (reduce premature NCD mortality, extend healthy life expectancy, mental health); Target 3.8 (UHC — essential medicines incl. longevity interventions if deemed essential) |
| **SDG 9** (Industry, Innovation, Infrastructure) | Target 9.5: upgrade tech capability, increase R&D spending, especially in developing countries |
| **SDG 10** (Inequalities) | Longevity must not become another rich/poor divider; COVAX as the model for fair distribution of scarce life-saving tech |
| **SDG 17** (Partnerships) | Target 17.6 (knowledge transfer, capacity building on STI), 17.9 (public-private-civil society partnerships), 17.17 (voluntary partnerships) — HIC-LMIC collaboration, tech-sharing, pooled aging-data consortia |

**Status:** SDGs are **not legally binding** but form a globally agreed agenda; they reinforce state obligations on health-for-all and equity, and give effect to the rights to health and science.

**Core pledge:** "Leave no one behind" directly contradicts a two-tier humanity of the long-lived and the left behind (see [Longevity Divide](longevity-divide.md)).
